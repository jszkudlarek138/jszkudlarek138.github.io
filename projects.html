Vehicle Training Controls

D'Angelo Technologies LLC · Mechanical Engineering Intern

SolidWorks · LightBurn · 3D Printing · Laser Cutting · Assembly

Project Overview

I helped develop lower-cost physical control units used for vehicle training. My work combined CAD, rapid prototyping, mechanical design, electrical assembly, and production documentation.

My Contributions

  • Redesigned a vehicle-interface model in SolidWorks and created laser-cut overlays in LightBurn, producing four training units in one week for planned use across additional vehicles.
  • Designed and 3D printed a mechanical linkage connecting a vehicle gear selector to a potentiometer.
  • Replaced failure-prone snap-fit controller caps with a more durable press-fit design, eliminating breakage and improving retention.
  • Assembled and wired 25 controllers containing 250 total buttons across multiple switch configurations.
  • Produced engineering drawings documenting button locations and switch configurations so other technicians could assemble the units consistently.

Fall 2026

Coffee Portafilter Reverse Engineering

Engineering Design Course Project

Reverse Engineering · Manufacturing · Cost Analysis · Materials

Objective

My team disassembled and analyzed a commercial coffee portafilter to determine how its components function, identify likely materials and manufacturing processes, and estimate its production cost at a volume of 1,000 units.

Engineering Process

  • Documented the product's components, dimensions, and assembly.
  • Evaluated likely processes including polypropylene and TPE injection molding as well as machining of stainless-steel components.
  • Considered material, labor, tooling, facility, assembly, and marketing costs in the production estimate.
  • Estimated a retail price of $35 and a production cost of about $20 per unit at a quantity of 1,000.
